## Rules

- Count cash when it is received, not when it is promised.
- Subtract platform fees, payment fees, tools, and taxes.
- Do not mix personal savings with side-hustle capital.
- Do not include borrowed money, leverage, or unrealized gains as cash.
- Keep evidence for every deduction and payout.
