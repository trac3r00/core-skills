# Lane playbooks and red flags

Platform terms, fees, and eligibility change. Verify the current terms at the source before relying on
them. This file is a routing map, not a price list.

## Freelancing platforms

| Platform | Best for | What to verify before applying |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upwork | Broad services, longer contracts | Fees, Connects, payment protection, ID checks |
| Contra | Commission-free positioning, direct clients | Current fee model, payout rails |
| Toptal | Senior specialists | Screening process, exclusivity, rates |
| Fiverr | Packaged, repeatable deliverables | Tier levels, fees, response-time expectations |
| Niche job boards | Specific industries and skills | Posting recency, budget, decision maker |
| Direct outreach | Highest margin, no marketplace fee | Contract, deposit, scope, payment terms |

### Offer checklist

- Buyer: a specific role or business, not "everyone".
- Problem: one painful job with a cost of not solving it.
- Deliverable: what the buyer receives, in what format.
- Proof: a sample, case study, or public artifact.
- Price: a range with the basis; deposit and payment schedule.
- Boundary: revisions, timeline, what is excluded.

### Red flags

- Unpaid "test projects" that are actually production work.
- Payment outside the platform with no contract.
- Scope that expands before the first payment.
- Requests to misrepresent experience or provide fake reviews.
- A client who refuses to name the decision maker or budget.

## Bug bounty programs

| Platform | Use | Verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HackerOne | Public and private programs | Scope, policy, disclosure, payout |
| Bugcrowd | Public and private programs | Scope, priority, duplicates |
| Intigriti | European programs | Scope, safe harbor, payout |
| YesWeHack | European and global programs | Scope, rules, payout |
| Vendor programs | Direct relationships | Written authorization, safe harbor, contact |

### Before any test

- Written scope lists the asset you will test.
- Rules allow the technique you plan to use.
- The program has a safe-harbor or authorization statement.
- You know the prohibited data, availability, and privacy constraints.
- You have a separate research account and a way to redact evidence.

### Never

- Test an asset outside the scope, even if it shares infrastructure.
- Access, modify, or exfiltrate other users' data.
- Run denial-of-service, brute force, or destructive tests without explicit permission.
- Use social engineering, phishing, or physical intrusion.
- Publish before the disclosure deadline.

## Online hackathons

| Source | Use | Verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Devpost | Large online hackathons | Eligibility, judging, IP, deadlines |
| MLH events | Student and community events | Eligibility, code of conduct |
| Company challenges | Sponsor-specific prizes | IP, employment restrictions |
| Kaggle competitions | Data and ML | Rules, prize terms, team limits |

### Submission checklist

- Repo with a license and setup instructions.
- Demo video under the time limit.
- Write-up of the problem, approach, and result.
- Team eligibility and contribution statement.
- Required sponsor integrations and disclosures.

## Productized service and content

- Pick a repeatable deliverable with a fixed scope and a clear buyer.
- Template the delivery before adding customers.
- Price the outcome, not the hours.
- Do not promise results you cannot reproduce.
- Content compounds slowly; measure email subscribers, qualified leads, or product usage, not vanity
  views.

## Stocks and crypto

This is a risk map, not investment advice.

| Step | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Fund | Only earned side-hustle cash, after tax set-aside and emergency buffer |
| Leverage | Never margin, loans, credit cards, or "yield" products with hidden leverage |
| Diversification | Broad, low-cost funds before single assets |
| Crypto | Small speculative allocation only; expect loss; keep records |
| Records | Every deposit, withdrawal, fee, and taxable event |
| Advice | A qualified local professional for the user's tax and legal situation |

Red flags:

- Guaranteed returns, "risk-free" yield, or a fixed daily percentage.
- Paid signal groups, referral schemes, or pressure to recruit.
- A platform that cannot explain custody, fees, or withdrawal terms.
- A request to deposit before the user has earned the money.

## Evidence and approval

- Every opportunity gets a URL, date checked, eligibility, payout terms, and deadline.
- Every estimate gets a range and a source.
- Account creation, applications, client messages, and spending require explicit approval of the
  exact action and destination.
- Do not run automated scanning, mass messaging, or scraping against a platform without permission.
